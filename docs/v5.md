The player's feedback on v4, and what changes. Everything below is fixed so the parts can be built at the same time.
Anything not written here stays as v4 built it (docs/v4.md), or is the owner's call.

## 2. The game loop: matched fights, no stages

- No stages, waves, stars, wave bar or STAGE tab.
- BATTLE finds an opponent: a short matchmaking moment (searching, then FOUND) showing both armies, the opponent's
  name with a small BOT tag (bots must be clearly AI for Google Play) and both POWER numbers.
- The opponent is built to the player's army: about the same POWER (a difficulty factor that eases after losses and
  rises a little on win streaks) and a similar fighter count, from the cast near the player's own rungs, with a
  THEME so fights stay interesting (e.g. a swarm of low rungs, a few heavy hitters, glass cannons, tanks, a mirror of
  your own lineup, a mixed squad). Every 5th match is a CHAMPION match: a tougher team led by a crowned boss unit
  (PetLook.boss) with double rewards.
- Rewards: a win gives cash (plus a share per knock-out) and XP, sometimes gems (always on a champion win); a loss
  gives a little cash and XP. Win streaks add a bonus.
- Progress is a PLAYER LEVEL from XP. The top bar's centre pill shows LEVEL n with its XP bar, in the same pill style
  as cash and gems. Grid cells now unlock by cash, by player level (instead of stage), or by knock-outs.
- Everything else from v4 stays: cases (CASE for cash, ELITE for gems), the 4-slot tray, TRASH, the grid with
  locked cells, fusing and the x4 + x4 evolve, NEW UNIT reveals, the unit ladder panel, the guide.
- SaveState version 5. MIGRATE a version-4 save (keep pieces, cash, gems, opened cells, owned units, knock-outs,
  case counters, tutorial; drop the stage fields; start at level 1 or a level from the old stage) instead of
  resetting it.

## 3. The screen

- Keep the main-screen structure the player liked: the top bar, the arena (it grows during the fight while the grid
  slides away), the POWER tab, the grid, the tray, CASE and ELITE.
- Keep the cash and gems pills EXACTLY as they look now. Keep the POWER tab's look.
- Replace the stage parts: the centre pill becomes LEVEL n + XP bar; no STAGE tab; the arena's prep overlay shows a
  big BATTLE button (and the current win streak if any, and "CHAMPION MATCH" when the next is one).
- New overlays: the matchmaking moment, and a result panel that counts up the rewards (cash, KOs, XP bar filling,
  gems), names the MVP unit (most knock-outs) with its portrait, and has CONTINUE.
- THE ROLL: keep the plain empty background, but the prize now spins in a VERTICAL REEL from TOP TO BOTTOM, like the
  roll games on Roblox: a column of prize cards (units and items, each tinted by rarity) scrolls downward through a
  centre marker, fast at first, decelerating with ticks, overshooting slightly and settling on the prize, which then
  pops with its name, rung / class and a rarity burst. Neighbours above and below fade out. Tap to collect.
- COLOURS: richer and more inviting, not pale. A deeper base (for example a deep indigo or plum behind the panels,
  like the reference collection screenshot /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-zoopvp/ef6dad0a-b234-5ed3-bc57-4548e8186318/images/1.webp), saturated but soft-edged buttons (BATTLE a juicy green, CASE warm orange,
  ELITE purple), clear contrast. Not neon, not eye-straining. The arena keeps its look but may get a little more
  colour.

## 4. The battle: addicting and satisfying

The fight must feel juicy and premium, not cheap:
- A boxing-bell intro: both corners pop in, "READY ... FIGHT!" with a bell DING-DING and a crowd roar.
- Every hit lands: a white hit flash on the victim, squash and stretch, knock-back with a dust puff, small hit-stop
  and a camera shake scaled to the blow, a popping damage number (bigger and gold on a crit, with "CRIT!"), and comic
  impact words on big blows (POW! BONK! WHAM! SMASH!).
- Knock-outs: the loser is launched spinning out with stars (some fly into the crowd), coins burst out and fly up to
  the cash pill; a KO counter and combo calls ("DOUBLE KO!", "TRIPLE KO!", "RAMPAGE!"), each with its own sound;
  the crowd waves and roars on big moments.
- Small health bars over units once they are hurt; a team health tug bar at the top.
- The last knock-out plays in slow motion with a zoom, then "VICTORY!" / "DEFEAT" and the MVP.
- Deaths stay funny with no blood; marks stay on the pitch for the fight.
