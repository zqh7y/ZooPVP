## 1. The units: 12 original characters on one ladder

Pets become a varied cast of original characters (plants, animals, people, a rock), in a chunky cartoon style: a
thick dark outline, flat colours with one soft shade, a big head on a small body, very few details, lots of
personality. Our own designs and names: never a copy of another game's units. The cast is still the ladder from
docs/pets.md (12 rungs, rung = the unit's class, x4 + x4 evolves into the next rung's unit x1), and each character
keeps the trait of the pet on its rung, re-flavoured:

| rung | id | name | look (for the artist) | trait (from docs/pets.md) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | sprout | Sprout | a tiny seedling kid, two leaves on its head, stubby legs | swarm biter: +10% damage per nearby Sprout (up to +30%) |
| 2 | buzzy | Buzzy | a round little bee in a pilot cap and goggles | flying poisoner: stings poison; flies over its own lines |
| 3 | ninja | Ninja | a small hooded ninja kid, only the eyes show | evasive: dodges 30% of hits |

- In the code, keep the internal names (PetType, PETS, petArt, PetLook, SquadSpec.pet ...) to save time; only the ids
  and the display names change. `PetType = 'sprout' | 'buzzy' | 'ninja' | 'snailor' | 'roo' | 'cacto' | 'pogo' | 'briar' | 'angler' | 'sumo' | 'rhino' | 'boulder'`.
  The UI calls them "units".
- Weapons (knife, bat, shield, spear) and gear (helmet, sneakers, chili) stay, with their E..SSS classes, redrawn in
  the same chunky outline style.
- Team: yours wear a blue accent (a scarf, a headband, a cap, a cape: whatever fits the character), the enemy's a
  red one. Same drawing, only the accent changes. Readable at the size they fight at.
- The enemy army is made of the same cast (red accents). Wave 10 of every stage adds a BOSS: a giant (about 1.8x)
  version of a high-rung unit with a crown and much more health. PetLook gets `boss?: boolean`.

## 2. The game: stages and waves, no trophies, no matchmaking

Like Bunny Brigade (github.com/zqh7y/BunnyBrigade, cloned at /home/user/BunnyBrigade, read its README), in our
concept:
- No trophies, leagues, trophy road, matchmaking screen, BOT names or BOT tag. Progress is STAGE n, WAVE m of 10.
  Wave 10 is the boss wave.
- A wave is one automatic army-vs-army fight on the arena: your army (from the grid) against the wave's enemy
  army, built from the stage and wave so it is a fair but rising challenge (a recommended power, shown on the
  START WAVE button, like Bunny Brigade).
- Between waves: PREP time. The grid, tray and cases are open; START WAVE starts the fight (an AUTO toggle, like
  Bunny Brigade, starts it after a few untouched seconds). During a wave they are locked.
- Win a wave: a wave-clear ribbon with the cash earned, then prep for the next. Win wave 10: a stage-clear party
  with 1 to 3 stars (by how much of your army survived) and gems, then the next stage. Lose a wave: a soft defeat
  panel, and you retry the same wave (nothing is taken away).
- Money: knock-outs and wave clears give CASH; stage clears give GEMS. CASE costs cash (the price climbs a little
  each time); ELITE costs gems (never E-class, better units). The odds improve with the stage.
- The grid is 5x5, but like Bunny Brigade it starts small (the middle 3x3 open) and the locked cells open with cash,
  on reaching a stage, or after enough knock-outs (each locked cell shows how). The tray (bench) has 4 slots
  (BENCH_SLOTS = 4). TRASH sells a piece for a little cash.
- The first-time guide still teaches with the starter kit (Sprout, knife, helmet) and the first fuse (Sprout +
  Sprout -> x2).
- SaveState version 4; any older save resets.

## 3. One main screen, like Bunny Brigade's

Top to bottom (540 x 1170 logical):
- top bar: cash, a one-line WAVE m/10 bar with the boss's head at its end, gems; a STAGE n tab under it; the sound
  button and AUTO somewhere sensible;
- the ARENA: during prep, a shorter view with your army standing in its rows and the START WAVE button (with the
  recommended power) over it; when the wave starts, the arena smoothly grows to fill most of the screen (the grid
  slides down, mostly out of view, locked) so the fight is big and readable, and shrinks back after;
- the power tab above the grid, the grid, the tray between a small stats button and TRASH, then CASE and ELITE.
- The roll is plain: no slot machine, no case art. The screen dims to a soft empty background, the prize spins in
  the centre (units and items flicking past, slowing down), lands, pops with its name and rung / class, and a tap
  collects it.

## 4. The look

- The arena: flat and top-down (no perspective), a football pitch in a rounded-rectangle wall, muted, with simple
  crowd silhouettes in soft greys along both long sides holding little team flags, a scoreboard and a referee mascot
  at the top, a dugout at the bottom. Calm colours, clear readability. Our own design.
- Colours across the game are softer and balanced: lower saturation, no glaring gold or neon green, easy on the
  eyes, one gentle accent for the main actions. Keep one font (Barlow Condensed).
